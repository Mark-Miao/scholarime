The selected files provide validated CSV plot templates and deterministic checks for plotting
source, rendered PDF collisions, panel alignment, effective PDF text size, and SVG schematics.
Scholarime does not include the upstream image assets, generated examples, R runner, or AI image
generation workflow.

The field-level citation comparison workflow was informed by the upstream `nature-ref-verifier`
skill and reimplemented in Scholarime's own CiteVerify adapter.

Scholarime added a PNG export, configurable font family and size, and the unified `figure` command.
